Tempo Settings

Morning Shift · Connect Tempo to your own Power Automate flow.

Power Automate

Paste the HTTP POST URL from your flow’s “When an HTTP request is received” trigger.

Stored only in Chrome extension local storage on this browser profile. Tempo does not sync this URL to other browsers.

Leave the field blank and save to remove the stored URL.

Break schedule

Start times used by Scheduled mode, in Philippine time. Snack Break lasts 30 minutes and Lunch lasts 1 hour. Scheduled Learning Time starts at its own time, pauses for lunch, then continues.

Your Power Automate flow must use the times Tempo sends. If your flow still has fixed times in its scheduled steps, it keeps using those. See “Scheduled cases” in the README.

Time In messages

Add the messages for the end-of-day “byebye” post. When you press Time In, Tempo picks one at random and sends it to your flow. With only one message it is always used. Leave everything blank to use the flow’s default text.

Up to 20 messages, 200 characters each. Plain text only.

Keep your HTTP URL private. Masking hides it on screen; local storage is not encrypted. Saving settings does not trigger your flow.
